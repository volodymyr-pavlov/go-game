Privacy

This is a personal project. Volodymyr Pavlov runs it and is responsible for the personal data described on this page (the controller under the GDPR). If you have a question about this page, send it through the contact page of my blog.

What This Game Collects

Nothing, unless you do something first. The game, its fonts and the engine all come from this site, and the engine runs inside your browser. Analytics runs only if you allow it. The leaderboard stores an entry only if you join it. Both are explained below.

Leaderboard

If you play as a guest, the game signs you in without a name, using Firebase Authentication. You get a random user ID that is kept in your browser. If you link an email and password to keep your nickname on another device, Firebase Authentication stores that email and a hash of the password. The email is not used for anything else, and no message is ever sent to it. When you sign in, Firebase Authentication also gets your IP address and browser details, so it can create and renew your sign-in. Firebase belongs to Google. Google may process this data in the United States under the Firebase Privacy and Security terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you claim a nickname, the game server stores these things: your user ID, your nickname, your rating, your wins and losses, the time of the last update, and one record for each finished game. A game record holds the user ID and nickname of your opponent, or the engine level (Gopher, Bear or Elephant), the result, how the game ended (by score, by resignation, or on time), your rating before and after the game, and the time. Your nickname, rating, wins and losses are public. Anyone who opens the game can see the leaderboard. The game server runs on Cloudflare (Workers, the D1 database and Durable Objects). Cloudflare is based in the United States and may process this data there under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Every request to the game server also reaches Cloudflare with your IP address and browser details. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by claiming a nickname (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Before you claim a nickname, open a room or join one, the game runs a check from Cloudflare Turnstile to tell a person from a script. Turnstile looks at signals from your browser and your IP address. It follows the same Cloudflare Privacy Policy. The legal basis is a legitimate interest in keeping the service free of abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The guest sign-in is kept in your browser's IndexedDB storage, so this device stays linked to your entry. If you clear the site data or use another device, you start with a new entry. You can delete your entry yourself with "Delete my entry" on your profile page. It removes the nickname, the rating, the game records and the sign-in, including a linked email. If you cannot get to the profile page any more, send the nickname through the contact page, and I will delete it.

Online Games

If you open or join a room to play against another visitor, the game server keeps the room in a Durable Object on Cloudflare. A room holds the user IDs and nicknames of both players, the moves, the result, and the time of the last move. Other signed-in visitors see the open rooms with the nickname of the host. A room that nobody joined is removed when the host leaves, or after ten minutes. The record of a finished game stays on the server. It is not removed when you delete your entry, so to have it removed, send the nickname of a player of that game through the contact page. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by opening or joining a room (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Google Analytics

If you allow it in the consent bar, this site loads Google Analytics to count visits. Google Analytics then sets cookies whose names start with _ga. It sends Google your IP address, your browser details, the pages you open, and the page you came from. Google may process this data in the United States under the Google Privacy Policy. Advertising features and Google Signals are turned off, so the data is not used for ads and is not linked across your devices. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If the site has no analytics set up, there is no bar and nothing is loaded.

Your choice, allow or decline, is kept in your browser's local storage, so the bar does not ask again. You can take back your consent at any time with the "Analytics choice" link under the game. It deletes the analytics cookies and asks you again.

Hosting

The game is hosted on Cloudflare (Workers static assets). When you open the game, Cloudflare gets your IP address and browser details. It may keep them in server logs to serve the site and to protect it from abuse. Cloudflare is based in the United States and processes this data under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. The legal basis is a legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How Long Data Is Kept

  • Your analytics choice stays in your browser's local storage until you clear it or click "Analytics choice".
  • Google Analytics keeps visit data for 14 months and then deletes it. The _ga cookies expire after two years, or earlier if you delete them or take back your consent.
  • Your guest sign-in stays in your browser until you clear the site data or sign out.
  • Leaderboard entries and game records are kept until you delete your entry or ask me to remove it, or until the leaderboard is shut down.
  • Open rooms are removed after ten minutes at most. The record of a finished game is kept until you ask me to remove it, or until the service is shut down.
  • Google and Cloudflare keep their server logs for as long as their privacy statements say.
